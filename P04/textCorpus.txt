Once upon a time, in a land far away, there existed a small village nestled between towering mountains and lush green forests. The villagers were hardworking people who thrived on farming and raising cattle. Their lives were simple but filled with happiness, and every year, they celebrated the bountiful harvest that blessed their lands.

In the heart of the village lived a young girl named Alina. Alina was curious and adventurous, always exploring the nearby woods and hills. Her father, a wise man, had taught her everything he knew about the land and the animals that roamed it. Alina was particularly fond of a large, old oak tree that stood at the edge of the forest. The tree, with its sprawling branches and thick trunk, seemed to hold centuries of wisdom within it. Many stories had been told about this oak tree, some saying it was enchanted, while others believed it to be home to magical creatures.
