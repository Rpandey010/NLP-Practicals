Natural language processing (NLP) is a subfield of linguistics, computer science, and artificial intelligence concerned with the interactions between computers and human language. Specifically, it focuses on how to program computers to process and analyze large amounts of natural language data. 

Challenges in NLP involve speech recognition, natural language understanding, and natural language generation. The ultimate objective of NLP is to enable computers to understand and generate human language, which is complex and rich in meaning.

The field of NLP is growing rapidly with advancements in machine learning, and it powers many applications we use daily like chatbots, translation tools, and voice assistants.
